🌾 Interactive Photo Crop Test

🎯 Test Objective

Verify that users can select photos and immediately crop them by dragging edges, similar to normal photo editing applications.

✨ New Features Implemented

  • Auto-Crop Mode: Crop overlay appears automatically when photo is selected
  • Edge Dragging: 8 resize handles (4 corners + 4 edges) for precise cropping
  • Visual Feedback: Dimmed areas outside crop bounds with grid lines
  • Center Drag: Move entire crop area by dragging the center
  • Real-time Updates: Live preview of crop changes
  • Double-click Apply: Quick finish gesture
  • Boundary Constraints: Crop area stays within image bounds

📋 Test Steps

Step 1: Open the Design Studio application
Expected: Application loads with left toolbar, canvas, and right panel visible
Step 2: Click "Photos" in the left toolbar
Expected: Photos panel opens in the right sidebar showing available images
Step 3: Drag any photo from the photos panel onto the canvas
Expected: Photo appears on canvas and is automatically selected
Step 3a: Click once on the photo (without dragging)
Expected: Photo is selected but does NOT move. It should stay in place with selection indicators.
Step 3b: Click and drag the photo (hold mouse down and move)
Expected: Photo moves only when you drag it (requires 5px movement before drag starts). Single clicks should not move the photo.
Step 4: Look at the selected photo (no crop overlay yet)
Expected: Photo is selected with blue selection border, but NO crop handles appear yet. The photo should have normal resize handles only.
Step 5: Click the "Crop" button in the top bar (crop icon)
Expected: NOW the crop border appears with blue crop handles (8 handles total):
• 4 Corner handles (squares) for proportional resize • 4 Edge handles (top, bottom, left, right) for single-direction resize • Dimmed overlay outside crop area • Grid lines for composition guidance • Dashed blue border around crop area
Step 6: Drag a corner handle to resize crop area
Expected: Crop area resizes proportionally, dimmed areas update in real-time
Step 7: Drag an edge handle to resize in one direction
Expected: Crop area expands/contracts in the specific direction only
Step 8: Drag the center of the crop area
Expected: Entire crop area moves without changing size
Step 9: Double-click inside the crop area OR click "Apply" in top bar
Expected: Image is cropped to the selected area, crop handles disappear

🔧 Technical Implementation

The interactive crop system uses Konva.js for canvas rendering with the following components:

useImageCrop Hook: • Auto-detects image selections • Manages crop state and boundaries • Handles coordinate transformations ImageCropOverlay Component: • 8 draggable Konva Rect handles • Real-time drag event handling • Visual feedback with dimmed overlays • Grid composition guidelines CanvasEngine Integration: • Auto-activates crop mode on selection • Renders crop overlay above images • Handles crop completion and cancellation

🐛 Troubleshooting

If crop handles don't appear:

  • Make sure only one image is selected (multi-selection disables crop)
  • Check browser console for any JavaScript errors
  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Verify the development server is running on port 3000
